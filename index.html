Where the numbers come from

Money saved = the battery replacement you put off + electricity you didn't waste.

CO₂ prevented = electricity not wasted × Malaysia's grid emissions + the battery you didn't need to make yet.

Treats worth = money saved ÷ RM 2.50 (a typical local snack or drink, rounded to whole items).
